FruitBlend24 case study: notebooks

12 months of orders from an online smoothie brand (4 kitchens, LINE MAN and Grab) turned into a P&L, a budget gap analysis, price and promo decisions, a Sep-Nov 2026 forecast and a costed action plan.

Start here

  • Live app The findings and the action plan, page by page.
  • Approach note How the work was done, the main choices and what to check next.
  • Decision log Every judgment call and assumption, with the reason and the impact.
  • GitHub repo Code, data, outputs and the Excel P&L.

Notebooks (run in this order)

  • 01 Data quality Can we trust the order data, and what does cleaning remove in rows and THB?
  • 02 Demand and pricing When, what and where do customers buy, and what does each cup earn at each price?
  • 03 Promotions Do the promo codes pay back, and which ones to kill, change or test?
  • 04 P&L and budget Where is profit lost against the budget, by item and by kitchen?
  • 05 Forecast and inventory How much to prep, what will Sep-Nov 2026 sell, and what is the profit in each scenario?
  • 06 Summary numbers Does every number in the summary match the outputs?
